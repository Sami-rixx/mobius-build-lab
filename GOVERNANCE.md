# Governance

This document establishes the leadership structure, decision-making processes, and participation guidelines for MÖBIUS Build Lab.

## Leadership

### Project Lead and Mentor

**Samuel M. Kariuki** serves as the Project Lead and Mentor for MÖBIUS Build Lab. In this role:

- Sets the overall vision and direction for the Build Lab
- Identifies and prioritizes learning opportunities and projects
- Assigns tasks and coordinates contributor activities
- Makes final decisions on project direction and technical approach when consensus cannot be reached
- Maintains the integrity and educational value of the Build Lab experience

### Decision Authority

The Project Lead retains final decision authority on:

- Project selection and prioritization
- Project direction and scope
- Architectural decisions for Build Lab projects
- Task assignment and contributor onboarding
- Interpretation and enforcement of these governance guidelines

### Maintainers

The Project Lead may designate **Maintainers** — trusted contributors who assist with day-to-day project operations. Maintainers:

- Review and approve Pull Requests
- Merge approved PRs into `main`
- Assist with Issue triage and assignment
- Provide guidance to contributors

Maintainers are designated by the Project Lead and serve at their discretion. The Project Lead retains authority to override or reverse any Maintainer action.

## How Project Direction is Established

1. **Vision Setting:** The Project Lead defines the overall vision and educational objectives
2. **Project Identification:** The Project Lead identifies potential projects that serve learning goals
3. **Community Input:** Contributors may propose ideas through GitHub Issues
4. **Evaluation:** Proposals are evaluated based on learning potential, feasibility, and alignment with Build Lab goals
5. **Selection:** The Project Lead selects projects and defines high-level objectives

## How Tasks are Assigned

1. **Issue Creation:** Tasks are created as GitHub Issues by maintainers or proposed by contributors
2. **Triage:** Issues are reviewed for clarity, scope, and alignment with project goals
3. **Prioritization:** Issues are prioritized based on project needs and learning value
4. **Assignment:** The Project Lead or designated maintainer assigns Issues to contributors
5. **Tracking:** Assigned work is tracked through the Issue and collaborator profiles

## How Technical/Product Decisions are Made

### For Attention Mirror (Project #001)

Given that Attention Mirror is in the **early discovery and pre-development stage**, decisions follow this process:

1. **Problem Identification:** A need or question is identified
2. **Research:** Options and considerations are researched and documented
3. **Discussion:** Contributors discuss alternatives in Issues or dedicated decision documents
4. **Proposal:** A recommendation is made with reasoning
5. **Decision:** The Project Lead makes the final decision, documented in [`docs/projects/attention-mirror/decisions.md`](./docs/projects/attention-mirror/decisions.md)

### Decision Documentation

All significant technical and product decisions are recorded in the project's `decisions.md` file, including:

- The decision made
- The date it was made
- The rationale and alternatives considered
- The person who made the decision
- Any open questions or follow-up items

## How Contributors Participate

### Joining the Build Lab

1. Review this document and [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
2. Express interest by commenting on relevant Issues
3. Complete assigned tasks according to the workflow
4. Create a collaborator profile under `docs/collaborators/` using the provided template

### Contributor Responsibilities

- Follow the workflow defined in [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
- Document your work and reasoning
- Validate and understand AI-assisted work before submitting
- Maintain your collaborator profile with current status and activities
- Communicate openly and responsively

### Contributor Rights

- Request clarification on any aspect of the project
- Propose new ideas through GitHub Issues
- Receive fair and constructive feedback on your work
- Have your contributions reviewed and considered for merging
- Leave the project at any time

### Contributor Departure

If a contributor becomes inactive or leaves the project:

- **No action required:** Contributors may leave at any time without penalty
- **Profile updates:** Contributors are encouraged but not required to update their profile status
- **Work ownership:** All contributions remain part of the project under the existing license
- **Access:** Repository access is not automatically revoked unless there are security or policy concerns

## How Disagreements are Handled

1. **Discussion First:** Disagreements are discussed openly and respectfully
2. **Evidence-Based:** Decisions are based on facts, research, and project goals
3. **Escalation:** If discussion does not resolve the disagreement, escalate to the Project Lead
4. **Final Decision:** The Project Lead makes the final decision
5. **Documentation:** The decision and reasoning are documented for future reference

## Collaboration vs. Contribution vs. Founding

### Collaboration

Participation in MÖBIUS Build Lab is open to anyone interested in learning to build real products with AI. Collaboration involves:

- Contributing to discussions and documentation
- Completing assigned tasks
- Learning from the process and from others
- Following the established workflow and guidelines

### Contribution

Contributions are the concrete outputs you produce as part of the Build Lab:

- Code changes
- Documentation
- Research findings
- Design proposals
- Review feedback

All contributions are valuable and recognized, but they do not automatically confer additional rights or privileges beyond participation.

### Founding, Equity, and Revenue

**Important:** Participation in MÖBIUS Build Lab, including significant contributions to projects like Attention Mirror, **does not automatically grant** any of the following:

- Equity or ownership in any resulting products or companies
- Co-founder status or title
- Revenue rights or profit sharing
- Intellectual property rights beyond standard open-source contributions

The Project Lead reserves the right to determine the structure, ownership, and commercial arrangements for any projects that may emerge from the Build Lab. These arrangements, if any, will be established through separate, explicit agreements.

Any future founding team, equity distribution, or revenue-sharing arrangements will be:

- Clearly communicated and separately documented
- Based on explicit agreements, not implied by participation
- Established through proper legal and financial channels
- Transparent to all affected parties

## Future Formal Arrangements

If a project reaches a stage where formal founding, incorporation, or commercialization is appropriate, the Project Lead will:

1. Communicate intentions clearly to all active contributors
2. Establish transparent criteria for any founding roles or equity distribution
3. Create separate, explicit agreements for any commercial arrangements
4. Ensure all parties enter into arrangements knowingly and voluntarily

Until such explicit agreements exist, **contributors should assume that participation grants no equity, ownership, or revenue rights**.

## Questions or Concerns

If you have questions or concerns about governance:

1. Review this document carefully
2. Ask for clarification in a relevant GitHub Issue
3. Request a discussion by creating an Issue with the "decision/discussion" template
4. Contact the Project Lead directly for sensitive matters

## Amendments

This governance document may be amended by the Project Lead. Significant changes will be communicated to active contributors.
